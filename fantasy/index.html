What is Fantasy?
The basicsFantasy is a game where you act as the manager of an imaginary football team based on real players from real teams. The better they perform in reality, the more points you earn in Fantasy.
How do you play?Buy and transfer players, manage your budget, and set your starting XI. Follow real-life performances to decide who to start, bench, or transfer — and when.
When does it start?Fantasy leagues start alongside real ones. The most popular leagues run from August to May, but you can join anytime during the season.
